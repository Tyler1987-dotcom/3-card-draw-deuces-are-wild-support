# Privacy Policy

**3 Card Draw Deuces Are Wild**

**Effective Date:** October 8, 2026

## Overview

3 Card Draw Deuces Are Wild is designed with user privacy in mind. The app does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

## Information Stored on Your Device

The app allows you to enter a player name and keeps game statistics such as:

* Hands played
* Wins
* Losses
* Ties
* Win percentage
* Best hand

This information is stored locally on your device.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information from the app.

The app does not require:

* An account or login
* An online connection
* Location information
* Contacts
* Photos
* Camera access
* Microphone access
* Advertising identifiers
* Payment information

## Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or online user accounts.

## Data Sharing

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Player profile information and game statistics remain stored locally on the user's device until the user changes or resets them or removes the app.

Deleting the app removes its locally stored data according to the device's normal app deletion behavior.

## Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults because it does not collect or transmit personal information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If the privacy practices of the app change, this privacy policy may be updated to reflect those changes.
