# 3 Card Draw Deuces Are Wild — Support

Welcome to the support page for **3 Card Draw Deuces Are Wild**.

## About the Game

3 Card Draw Deuces Are Wild is a simple three-card poker-style card game. You and an opponent are each dealt three cards from a standard 52-card deck, and each player has one opportunity to exchange up to two cards.

All four 2s are wild and can substitute for another card when determining the best possible hand. The actual 2 always remains displayed as a 2.

## Hand Rankings

1. Royal Flush
2. Straight Flush
3. Three of a Kind
4. Straight
5. Flush
6. Pair
7. High Card

A straight ranks higher than a flush because a three-card straight is statistically less common than a three-card flush.

## Privacy

The game does not require an account or login.

Player profile information and game statistics are stored locally on the device. The game does not require an online account or subscription.
